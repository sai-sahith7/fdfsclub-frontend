Tickets open. You hear first.

Pick a movie, a date and your theatres. We'll message you when tickets open. Free movie ticket alerts in India.

How it works

  1. Pick your movie. Choose your city and the movie you're waiting for, even before tickets open.
  2. Narrow it down. Pick a date, and any theatre or just your favourites. Filter by format, language, show time and price if you like.
  3. Get the message. We check every few minutes, day and night. When matching tickets open, you get a Telegram message and an email with a booking link.

Common questions

How quickly will I hear about tickets?
We check your alerts every few minutes, day and night, and message you as soon as tickets open for a matching show.
Do I need Telegram?
No. Every alert is sent by email. Telegram is optional, but it usually reaches you faster.
Does FDFSClub book tickets for me?
No. We tell you when tickets open and link you straight to the booking page, where you pick your seats and pay as usual.
What happens after an alert is sent?
Each alert is sent once, then moves to your past alerts. If the date passes without tickets opening, it expires on its own.
How many alerts can I have?
Up to 3 active alerts at a time. Once one is sent, expires or is cancelled, you can add another.
How much does it cost?
Nothing. FDFSClub is free. Sign in with Google to start creating alerts.

FDFSClub isn't affiliated with any ticketing site. We only tell you when tickets open.
